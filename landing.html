The problem

Important medical information gets lost between visits.

Paper prescriptions disappear. Medication names are forgotten. Doctors spend valuable consultation time rebuilding a history they have already heard before.

01

Rushed case-taking

02

03

Allergies, surgeries, and medication conflicts can be overlooked under pressure.

04

Broken continuity

Every new doctor and every follow-up risks starting from the beginning.
